Phase 1 sampled self-labeling commands

Meaning:
- sampled mode: every episode samples a different synthetic block problem from the real block file.
- fixed mode: repeats config_np_100.yaml and is smoke/debug only.
- all8: 8 heuristic candidates compete with rollout-samples agent candidates.
- pair-action hardmask_gaponly: length/thickness, block count, W/O gap, and absdev are not used; CUT_LTH > 1000 blocks cannot select Bay 24; score tuple is (steel_gap, cut_gap, bevel_gap, long_cut_bay24).
